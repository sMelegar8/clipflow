ClipFlow

A personal, single-user tool that prepares short vertical videos from rights-cleared sources and uploads them to its owner's YouTube channel.

Privacy PolicyTerms of Service

What it does

  • Builds short English videos from public-domain or openly licensed sources (for example NASA media, arXiv abstracts and Wikipedia facts), always citing the source.
  • Checks every video locally (format, audio, captions) before any upload.
  • Uploads only to the channel that its owner authorised, using the YouTube Data API with the owner's consent.

ClipFlow is not a public service: there are no user accounts and it is not offered to third parties.
